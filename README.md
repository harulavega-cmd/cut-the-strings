## Author’s note

The Godfather story is a man who holds every string. This book is the reverse. Cut the strings above your own shoulders — appetite, excuse, vanity, other people’s scripts — then accept the weight of what you chose.

A boy is not automatically a man. The tests rhyme across cultures: protect, provide, keep your word, endure pain without making it everyone else’s problem, leave the ground better than you found it.

If you want a costume of toughness, close the file. If you want a spine, do the thirty days at the end.

— Haru Lavega, Bogor, 2026

## The code (short)

Manliness is not volume, fashion, or contempt. It is self-command under load. Keep your word. Train the body. Do useful work. Tell the truth when it costs. Provide without advertising. Do not become the puppet or the puppeteer.

## Chapter X — The thirty-day manly challenge

Thirty days. Silent. No daily posting. One honest page in a notebook at the end.

**Standing rules**
- Wake at a fixed hour, including Sunday.
- No phone for the first thirty minutes.
- Train four days a week. Walk every day.
- One delayed hard conversation by Day 10.
- Miss a rule: restart that rule the same day. Do not burn the month.
- Night log: kept / broke / repaired.

**Days**
1. Inventory — body, money, word, family, work. Strong / Soft / Broken.
2. Write the man you intend to be in twelve months.
3. Cut one string. Ban that appetite for the rest of the month.
4. Train past the point you wanted to stop.
5. Clean one room and one money surface.
6. Speak half as much. Ask two questions first.
7. Score the week: wake, train, word, temper.
8. Track every spend. Save before night.
9. Ninety minutes on the craft that pays. Phone in another room.
10. Have the delayed conversation: fact, effect, request.
11. Serve the house or a neighbor with no post.
12. Read twenty pages of a serious life. Copy one decision.
13. Delay speech ten breaths when irritated.
14. Re-read Day 1. Correct the inventory in ink.
15. Fast one easy pleasure for daylight hours.
16. Teach one useful skill.
17. Long walk or extra sets. Stay when bored.
18. Send the apology or the payment you owe.
19. Three problems next quarter, first move for each.
20. One meal, phone gone, eyes up.
21. Score week three. Drop one vanity habit.
22–28. Keep the standing rules. Repair the same day.
29. Write one page: what you are now that you were not on Day 1.
30. Choose the four habits that survive next month. Start tomorrow.
