Author’s note

A boy is not automatically a man. A man is made by tests he does not schedule: protect, provide, keep your word, endure pain without making it everyone else’s problem, leave the ground better than you found it.

This book fuses ten works of history, biography, and self-mastery into one code, then ends in a 30-day challenge.
